Vector drawing without the learning curve.

No need for difficult professional drawing tools when you’ve got Vecterium X.

Vecterium X is a small, fast vector editor. The layout is the one you know from Scratch’s costume editor, with real layers added.

Runs on Rust, speed to the moon.

The app itself is written in Rust, so it starts fast and stays small.

Nine tools, all where you expect them.

Pick a tool on the left, draw in the middle, set colors along the top. Nothing is buried in a menu.

Select

Move, resize and rotate. Drag a box to pick several shapes.

Reshape

Drag points and curve handles. Click an edge to add a point.

Brush

Draw freehand. Strokes smooth themselves into clean curves.

Eraser

Drag across a shape to remove it.

Fill

Click a shape to recolor it.

Text

Click, type, press Enter. Double-click to edit later.

Line

Hold Shift to snap to 45 degrees.

Circle

Hold Shift for a perfect circle.

Rectangle

Hold Shift for a perfect square.

Layers that stay out of your way.

Hide a layer, lock it, rename it or drag it to a new spot. New shapes land in the layer you have selected, so your sketch never ends up inside your background.

Layers Click an eye to try it.

Saves as SVG, so nothing is locked in.

Your drawings are ordinary SVG files. Open them again here to keep editing, or take them anywhere else.

Save
SVG that remembers your layers and your editable points.
Export
PNG at 1x or 2x, or a clean SVG for other programs.
